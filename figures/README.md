# Figures — publication-ready

High-resolution PNG raster (4200 px wide, white background, ~600 DPI at 7 in) rendered
from the manuscript vector source. The editable vector `.svg` is included alongside each
PNG. At submission, upload the `Figure_N.png` files as separate figure files (or the
`.svg` vectors if the journal accepts vector art).

## Figure 1
- Files: `Figure_1.png` (4200×1641 px, 224 KB), `Figure_1.svg` (vector source)
- Caption: The three computational layers of healthcare IT, organized by the unit of
  information each makes computable. Layers accumulate rather than replace; layer 3 is
  proposed.
- Alt text: three stacked boxes, record at the base, clinical state above it, and
  clinical intent at the top, with an upward arrow indicating that each layer builds on
  the one below.

## Figure 2

- Caption: The ACR schema.
