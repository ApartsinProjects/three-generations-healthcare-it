Open access and charges (APC)

JAMIA is a hybrid (optional) open-access journal. Publishing under the standard subscription model carries no charge to authors: per the OUP author instructions, “if you do not select the open access option, your paper will be published with standard subscription-based access and you will not be charged.”

Choosing open access incurs an article processing charge (APC) set by OUP's current price list. Because the exact hybrid-JAMIA APC was not verified from a primary OUP price page at the time of writing, no figure is quoted here; consult the live OUP charges page for the current amount. (Note: the roughly US$3,600 figure that circulates online is for the separate fully-open-access sister journal JAMIA Open, not hybrid JAMIA.)

Many institutions hold OUP Read & Publish agreements that fund open-access publication for eligible corresponding authors; eligibility is determined by the corresponding author's affiliation and cannot be obtained by changing the corresponding author.
